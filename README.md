## What I work on

- **Learning engineering** — designing learning experiences around observable progress.
- **AI in education** — applying AI where it reduces repetitive work and improves feedback.
- **Teacher tools** — making classroom workflows clear and usable for educators.
- **Language learning** — building practical English-learning experiences for real-world communication.
- **Education systems** — connecting teaching, assessment, and learning evidence.

## My approach
